**Biography / 个人简介**<br>
I currently work with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6G-l4o0AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Xian-Sheng Hua</a> at Tongji University. I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University in 2021, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YG0DFyYAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Hanwang Zhang</a>, where I worked on computer vision, especially vision-language tasks and the distribution bias problem. Based on this research, I was selected as one of the world's <a href="https://topresearcherslist.com/Home/Search?AuthFull=tang%2C+kaihua" target="_blank">top 2% scientists</a>. Before that, I obtained dual master's degrees in Computer Science from the joint programme of Shanghai Jiao Tong University (advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yd58y_0AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Lizhuang Ma</a>) and Waseda University (advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-bQYetwAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Sei-Ichiro Kamata</a>), and my B.E. degree in Computer Science from the IEEE Pilot Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2015.

**Our Vision / 研究愿景**<br>
My research interests focus on the open-world generalization of multimodal models, 3D spatial perception and generation, creativity and divergent thinking in LLM/VLM, and embodied intelligence in extreme or hazardous environments. On the application side, out of personal passion, I also follow the directions below and am actively looking for students and partners who share these interests: AI-assisted game design, indie games, open-source agent projects, embodied intelligence for emotional companionship, etc. <span style="color: #e74c3c; font-weight: bold;">My vision is to use AI to push beyond the boundaries of human knowledge and capabilities. To create, not replace.</span><br>
我的研究兴趣主要集中于多模态模型的开放场景泛化性、3D空间感知与生成、LLM/VLM的创造力与发散性思维，以及极端/高危环境下的具身智能。在AI应用方面，出于个人兴趣，我也关注以下方向，并积极寻找对此感兴趣的学生与合伙人：AI辅助游戏设计、独立游戏、智能体开源项目、面向情感陪伴的具身智能等。<span style="color: #e74c3c; font-weight: bold;">我的愿景是用AI突破人类知识与能力的边界，去创造而非替代。</span>
